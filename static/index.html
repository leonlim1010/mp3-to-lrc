How to use SoniScript

  1. Upload an MP3 and select Transcribe All.
  2. Open your private LRC in the Editor to correct lyrics.
  3. Use Sync Tester to adjust timing, then download a backup.

Account and storage rules

  • Guest files are private but expire after 7 days. Clearing browser data can remove access.
  • Signed-in files are private and retained until you delete them.
  • MP3 uploads are temporary and deleted after processing; only LRC text is stored.

Usage rules

  • Guests: 3 transcriptions/day, maximum 10 minutes each.
  • Signed-in users: 10 transcriptions/day, maximum 15 minutes each.
  • Maximum upload size is 25 MB. Shared Groq capacity can temporarily become unavailable.
  • Only upload audio you are permitted to process. Automated or abusive use is prohibited.

AI transcription can contain mistakes, especially with noise, music, accents, or overlapping voices. Always review the result.
